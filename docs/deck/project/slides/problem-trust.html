Problem 2 · Verification

Who checks the robot data?

Crowdsourced demos can be bad in three ways.

FAKED

A script pretends to be a person

COPIED

One run, submitted again and again

SLOPPY

Jerky, idle or failed runs

Today, one company grades its own homework. Buyers just have to trust it.

TALOS

03

Problem two: verification. Once you crowdsource demos, some will be faked by scripts, copied and resubmitted, or simply sloppy. Today the platform that collects the data also judges it on its own servers, so buyers have to take its word. Verification is hard, and it should not depend on trusting one company.
