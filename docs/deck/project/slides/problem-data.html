TALOS

02

Hardware and funding are no longer the bottleneck in robotics; data is. Language models had the whole internet. Robots have a few hundred thousand hours of teleop data, and real teleop costs labs 90 to 200 dollars per usable hour. You cannot scrape it: a human has to demonstrate every task.
