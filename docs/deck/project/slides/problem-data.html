Problem one: collecting robot data. Robots learn by being shown, and that data is scarce: under a million hours exist, versus trillions of words for language models. Real teleop costs 90 to 200 dollars per usable hour. And it has to be robot data: an egocentric video of human hands has no joint angles or gripper commands, so it cannot replace teleoperation.
