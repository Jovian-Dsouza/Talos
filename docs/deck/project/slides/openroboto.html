The closest robotics subnet is OpenRoboto, SN80. It collects egocentric video: footage of human hands doing tasks. That needs a camera, films real homes, and has no robot joint angles or gripper commands, so it cannot replace teleop data. Talos collects real robot actions from just a phone, in simulated scenes with no privacy risk. And SN80 miners train robot models, so Talos can supply them.
