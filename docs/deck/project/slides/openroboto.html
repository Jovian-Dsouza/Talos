TALOS

09

OpenRoboto, subnet 80, is the closest robotics neighbour on Bittensor. It produces models: miners fine-tune pi zero point five, and it collects egocentric video, which needs a camera and films real homes. Talos collects teleop trajectories with actual robot actions, needs no hardware, has no faces or homes in it, and every run is replayed by validators. Where we do better: hardware-free, privacy-free, action-labelled and verified. And we fit together: SN80 miners need training data, and Talos can supply it.
