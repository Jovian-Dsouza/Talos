The solution

Bittensor solves both.

Problem 1 · Collection

Miners collect

Anyone with a phone drives a simulated robot. Pools pay them for every verified run.

Problem 2 · Verification

Validators verify

Independent validators replay and score every run. The chain records the verdict.

No hardware. No trust needed. Real robot actions.

TALOS

04

Bittensor already pays strangers to do work and pays others to check it. That maps exactly onto our two problems. Miners are pools that collect robot teleop data from people on their phones. Validators independently replay and score every run, and the result is recorded on chain. No hardware, no trust in a single company, and the data contains real robot actions.
