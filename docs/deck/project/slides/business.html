Who pays: VLA and foundation model labs first, because the LeRobot ecosystem is built around the SO-101. The offer goes free sample, then paid datasets, then custom bounties. The flywheel: a lab pays for a task, that task’s demand multiplier rises so pools shift effort to it, and revenue buys back the subnet’s alpha, which raises emissions. Unlike points, rewards follow what buyers actually pay for.
