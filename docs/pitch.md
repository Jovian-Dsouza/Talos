# Pitch outline

1. Problem: robot foundation models are bottlenecked on demonstration data, and crowdsourced data is
   only as good as its verification. Axis proves browser sim teleop scales (229k contributors) but
   quality is judged on a central server.
2. Solution: a Bittensor subnet where validators replay every recording themselves. Seeded tasks make
   copying and pre-recording fail. Phone controls give smooth data. Same action space as real SO-101.
3. Demo: judges drive the arm, the verdict arrives, the weights land on testnet, the real arm repeats
   the accepted run.
4. Why Bittensor: trustless verification by independent validators, pooled miners that recruit and
   pay humans, emissions steered by buyer demand multipliers, revenue funding alpha buybacks.
5. Market: VLA labs first. Offer an open sample, paid datasets and custom task bounties. No public
   price for browser sim data exists, which is what outreach is testing.
6. Competition: Axis (funded, big head start), PrismaX, BitRobot/FrodoBots, OpenRoboto SN80 as
   customer and neighbor.
7. Risks and answers: demand (outreach and open sample), sim to real gap (action space parity and
   real-arm replay, later policy improvement bonus), bots (seed binding now, human-likeness checks
   next), emissions dependency (revenue before mainnet at roughly 440 TAO).
8. Path: human-likeness detection, correction tasks on policy failures, bounty marketplace, more
   robots, policy-improvement bonus.
