How it works

  1. Sign in with Google

    We create your wallet and pay for your miner registration on the subnet. No crypto knowledge needed.

  2. Teleoperate the arm

    Pick a lab task and drive an SO-101 arm with on-screen joysticks. Runs in your browser, on any phone.

  3. Validators replay it

    Every run is replayed in the same physics engine. Fake or tampered data fails the replay and earns nothing.

  4. Data earns, buyers get LeRobot

    Validators set weights on chain by score. Accepted runs are exported as a LeRobot dataset for robot learning.

The score

score = 0.5 × time_score + 0.5 × smooth_score time_score is 1 when you finish within the task's reference time and falls as you take longer. smooth_score is higher for steady, low-jerk motion. Only runs that replay exactly, complete the task and are not duplicates are scored.
Runs on Bittensor testnet. Simulation and replay use MuJoCo 3.17.
