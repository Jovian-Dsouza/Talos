The problem

Robot data is hard
to get cheaply.

Chatbots learn from text on the internet. Robots cannot. Someone has to show a robot each move, and today that means real robots, trained operators and a big budget.

01

Scarce

There is very little robot training data in the world, and it cannot be copied from the web.

SUPPLY / LIMITED
02

Expensive

Collecting it needs a robot lab and paid operators, at up to $200 for one usable hour.

COST / HIGH
03

Closed

A few companies collect the data and check their own work. Everyone else has to take their word for it.

ACCESS / CENTRALIZED

The solution

A decentralized
data network.

  1. 01

    Drive on your phone

    Open a task and steer a simulated robot arm. It moves just like the real one.

  2. 02

    Send your run

    When you finish, your run goes to the network to be checked.

  3. 03

    RoboJev checks it

    Validators use RoboJev to score how good the run was. Bad or fake runs are rejected.

  4. 04

    Earn and build the dataset

    Good runs earn rewards and join a dataset that companies use to train robots.

Why a phone

A real robot,
in your pocket.

The phone runs the same physics as a real SO-101 robot arm. Anyone with a phone can make useful robot data and earn from it, without owning a robot.

Why Bittensor

Everyone is paid
for their part.

Bittensor rewards people for doing useful work and other people for checking it. That lets Talos run with no single company in charge.
