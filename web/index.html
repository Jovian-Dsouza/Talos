    How it works

    1. Sign in with Google

      We create your wallet and pay your miner registration on the subnet. No crypto setup.

    2. Drive the arm

      Pick a lab or warehouse job and steer an SO-101 arm with on-screen joysticks, right in your browser.

    3. Validators replay it

      Your run is re-simulated step by step. A run that was faked or edited does not replay, and earns nothing.

    4. Earn and build the dataset

      Verified runs earn you TAO through validator weights and are exported as LeRobot datasets.

    How a run is scored

    score = 0.5 × time_score + 0.5 × smooth_score Finish within the task's reference time for a full time score; it falls as you take longer. Steady, low-jerk motion earns a high smoothness score. Only runs that replay exactly, finish the task and are not copies of earlier runs are scored.
    Simulation and replay run on MuJoCo 3.17. TauNet is a Bittensor subnet.
